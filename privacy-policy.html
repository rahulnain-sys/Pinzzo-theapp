Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Pinzzo ("Pinzzo", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, contact us on WhatsApp or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number, delivery address and pincode.
  • Prescriptions & health information: images of prescriptions and any medicine or health details you share with us so we can fulfil your order.
  • Order details: items ordered, amount paid, delivery time and order history.
  • Payment information: UPI transaction reference or cash-on-delivery status. We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords.
  • Website usage: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, which our hosting provider may log for security and performance.

2. How we use your information

  • To verify prescriptions through our licensed pharmacist and process your order.
  • To deliver your order and contact you about it (for example, to confirm price, availability or delivery).
  • To respond to your questions, complaints and requests.
  • To keep records required by law, including the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and its Rules.
  • To improve our service and, only with your consent, to send you offers or updates. You can opt out at any time.

3. WhatsApp communication

When you order or chat with us on WhatsApp, your messages and attachments are also handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy. We only use what you send us for the purposes described here.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • Partner pharmacies & pharmacists who verify and dispense your medicines.
  • Delivery partners, who receive only your name, phone number and address.
  • Service providers such as hosting and payment providers, bound by confidentiality.
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and prescription records for as long as required under applicable drug laws and for tax and accounting purposes. Other personal data is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures including restricted access, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and confidentiality obligations for staff and partners. Only authorised staff and pharmacists can view prescriptions. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct, update or delete your data (subject to legal record-keeping requirements).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing messages.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

With your consent, our website uses Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use (for example, pincode checks and WhatsApp taps), and which link or advert brought them. This helps us improve the site. We do not use advertising cookies.

We never send your prescription, medicine names, notes, name or phone number to Google Analytics. Google Analytics does not store your IP address. When you arrive from one of our campaign links, we remember the campaign name on your device for 30 days and add it as a short "Ref" line to your WhatsApp message, so we know which campaign helped you.

You can accept or decline when you first visit, and change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

9. Children

Our services are meant for users aged 18 and above. Orders for children must be placed by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date.
