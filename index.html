How do I place an order?

Upload your prescription on this website or send its photo to us on WhatsApp. Our pharmacist will confirm the order and price, and we'll deliver it.

Do I need a prescription?

Only for prescription medicines. Baby care, wellness and other OTC products can be ordered by just sending us the product names.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are delivered in about 30 minutes after the order is confirmed.

What discount do I get?

You get up to 10% off on your order. The exact amount is shared when the pharmacist confirms your order.

How can I pay?

UPI or cash on delivery.
