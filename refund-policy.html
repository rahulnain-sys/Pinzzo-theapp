Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

We want you to be happy with every order. This policy explains when you can cancel an order, which items can be returned, and how refunds work.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: you can cancel free of charge by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling us.
  • After dispatch: because we deliver within minutes, orders usually cannot be cancelled once the rider has left. You may refuse the delivery only if the items are wrong or damaged.
  • Pinzzo may cancel an order if a medicine is out of stock, the prescription is invalid, or the address is outside our service area. Any amount paid will be refunded in full.

2. Items you can return

Please check your order when it arrives. You can request a return or replacement within 48 hours of delivery if:

  • You received a wrong product, strength or quantity.
  • The product is damaged, leaking or tampered with.
  • The product is expired or close to expiry (less than 3 months) without being told in advance.
  • An item is missing from your order.

Share a photo of the product, packaging and invoice/bill on WhatsApp so we can resolve it quickly.

3. Items that cannot be returned

  • Medicines that were delivered correctly and in good condition (for safety, medicines cannot be resold).
  • Temperature-sensitive products such as insulin, vaccines and other refrigerated items, unless damaged or wrong.
  • Opened or used products, including baby food, personal care and hygiene items.
  • Products without their original packaging, label or batch number.

4. Refunds

  • Once a return or cancellation is approved, we refund the amount for the affected items.
  • UPI payments: refunded to the same UPI account within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on delivery: refunded by UPI or bank transfer to an account you share with us, within 5–7 business days.
  • Where possible, we may offer a quick replacement instead of a refund, if you prefer.
Tip: Please check the medicine name, strength and expiry date in front of the delivery partner. It makes resolving any issue much faster.
