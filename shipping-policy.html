Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated:

Pinzzo delivers medicines and pharmacy essentials fast, right to your door. Here's how delivery works.

1. Where we deliver

We currently deliver across serviceable pincodes in Gurugram, Haryana. You can check your pincode on our home page. We plan to expand to the rest of Delhi NCR soon.

2. Delivery time

  • Most orders are delivered in about 30 minutes after the pharmacist confirms the order.
  • Delivery hours: .
  • Times are estimates and may be longer during heavy rain, traffic, festivals or high demand. We'll keep you updated on WhatsApp.
  • If a medicine is not immediately available, we'll tell you the expected time before confirming.

3. Delivery charges

Any delivery charge, if applicable, is shared with you before the order is confirmed. There are no hidden charges.

4. Receiving your order

  • Please keep your phone reachable so our delivery partner can contact you.
  • For some prescription medicines, the delivery partner may ask to see the original prescription.
  • Please check the products, quantity and expiry date at the time of delivery.

5. Safe & private packaging

Orders are packed in sealed, tamper-evident and discreet packaging. Temperature-sensitive medicines such as insulin are carried in insulated packs to maintain the cold chain.

6. Failed or missed delivery

If we cannot reach you or the address is incorrect, we'll try to contact you. If delivery isn't possible, the order may be cancelled and any payment refunded as per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
